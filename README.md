# RED SHIFT

A browser 3D survival FPS. Time slows to 1/30 speed while the player stands still.
Enemies arrive in waves; rifle elites wear a face sticker and require two hits.

Controls: WASD to move, mouse to aim, left click to fire, Q or right click to throw,
Escape to pause, R to restart, and M to mute.

The Pistol_2 and AssaultRifle2_2 models come from Quaternius's Ultimate Gun Pack
(CC0 1.0). The enemy face image was supplied by the game's owner.
